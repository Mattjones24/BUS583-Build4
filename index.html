A customer assistant that knows where to stop

This assistant handles routine questions for Tallow Creek Heating & Air, a fictional residential heating and cooling company. It grew out of my Week 1 assistant, which learned not to invent facts.

All customers, records and phone numbers in this project are synthetic. No real mailbox, CRM or customer information is connected.

The authority line

The assistant's freedom depends on how much a mistake could cost and whether the business can take it back.

Act alone

Small, correctable answers from written policy or a verified record.

Draft for approval

Material or partly reversible actions, including anything that moves money or makes a promise.

Escalate, do not act

Severe or irreversible situations, including safety, legal, public-complaint and liability demands.

The limit

These controls are written instructions in a Claude Project with no connected tools. A live deployment would need the approval step enforced by the systems that send messages or move money.

Built as a course project for BUS 583. Tallow Creek Heating & Air is not a real business.
